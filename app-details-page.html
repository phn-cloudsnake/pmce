About this App

This app automatically shoots a series of still images at intervals then combines them into one movie. It's so easy to use: just select a time-lapse mode that most closely resembles what you are shooting and let the camera do the rest! The proper settings are automatically made for you, eliminating the need for time-consuming trial and error. Eight time-lapse themes are available: Cloudy Sky, Night Sky, Night Scene, Sunset, Sunrise, Miniature, Standard, Custom. You can choose to save the still images as a series of photos instead of a movie. Using Angle Shift Add-on, you can easily add pan, tilt, zoom and other effects to your time-lapse images.
